Privacy Policy – YouTube Otomasyon

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What the app does

YouTube Otomasyon is a private tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to upload videos, edit titles and descriptions, manage playlists and read analytics for their own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's YouTube channel data: videos, playlists, channel details and YouTube Analytics reports. It does not access data of any other user.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to manage the owner's own channel.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
